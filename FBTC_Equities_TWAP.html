Methodology: Time-Weighted Gravity

TWAP is the expanding average price paid by the market since the 2020 Bitcoin liquidity epoch. Premium versus TWAP measures whether the instrument is trading above or below that time-weighted cost basis. Gravity Risk rescales that premium against the asset's own historical premium range, assigning levels 1-10 from accumulation to distribution.
