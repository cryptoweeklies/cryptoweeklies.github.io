3. Data Collection, Scope of Access, and Storage Rules

CryptoWeeklies maintains a transparent, privacy-first technical infrastructure. We adhere to the following data scopes and storage limits:

  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect, request, harvest, log, or track full names, usernames, facial profiles, browsing histories, location patterns, or contact methods from visitors to this website or from users of our mobile apps. Browsing this site and using the apps requires no account and no identification of any kind. The developer API described in Section 9 is the one place where a name and an email address exist at all, and that section says plainly who holds them.
  • API Metadata Accessed: The read-only implementation of our automated script accesses public YouTube video metrics, including public video titles, unique alphanumeric Video IDs, public upload timelines/timestamps, and auto-generated English text captions/transcripts.
  • Account Credentials: This platform completely rejects, avoids, and forbids requesting or saving private user login passwords or secure Google account permissions.
  • Storage Limitations: Public text caption data is handled ephemerally and processed in temporary runtime memory caches. No localized personal user metrics or identity tables are stored indefinitely on our networks.

4. Processing Logic and Data Sharing Prohibition

All aggregated third-party data is executed programmatically strictly to support prominent, user-facing analytical features:

Captured text captions and timestamps are converted into local text inputs strictly to run zero-temperature language models that automatically filter speech into mathematical context blocks (Current Structures, Macro Risk Bands, and Target simple moving averages) for educational quantitative research. All metrics are compiled on an aggregated, anonymized basis to display community traffic trends across historical cycles.

6. Local Data Deletion Procedure and Authorization Revocation (Policy #:III.A.2h)

Because CryptoWeeklies operates on a strict read-only structural database configuration, we do not capture or retain user credentials or personalized database tables on our servers. 8. Mobile Applications (iOS and Android)

CryptoWeeklies is also available as a mobile app. The apps read the same public data feeds as this website and require no account, sign-in or subscription. The following applies specifically to the apps:

  • On-device preferences: Your watchlist, alerts, notification and widget preferences, first-run state, and cached readings are stored only on your device. They never leave it, and deleting the app deletes them.
  • Push notifications: If you enable notifications, the app registers an anonymous device push token with Firebase Cloud Messaging so that data-refresh notifications can be delivered. The token identifies the app installation, not you, and is not used for advertising or tracking.
  • Crash reporting (iOS only): The iOS app uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and a random installation identifier when the app fails. Crash data is used solely to diagnose and fix defects. It is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising.
  • Usage analytics (iOS and Android): The apps use Firebase Analytics to record a small set of usage events: which screen was opened, which asset’s model deck was opened and from where, whether an alert was armed (never the level you chose), and whether an assistant question came from the suggested list (never the question itself). No free text is ever recorded, and no identifier of ours is attached — Firebase’s own random installation identifier is the whole of it. Tickers you open are recorded because they are public facts about a market, not about you. This data is used only to understand which parts of the app are used; it is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising.
  • Ask AI (iOS and Android): Every question is answered first from CryptoWeeklies’ own published research. Only a question that asks for an explanation — why, how, compare and the like — is also sent, as text, through our Cloudflare service to Groq, which writes a short answer on top of that research (see Section 10). A short lookup such as “BTC risk” is not sent to Groq. Nothing identifying you or your device is sent with a question, and the iOS app asks your permission before the first question is sent.
  • What the apps do not collect: No names, email addresses, contacts, precise locations, browsing histories, portfolios, or holdings. Firebase Analytics infers an approximate region (country or city) from the network address of each usage event; the apps never ask for or read your location. The apps contain no third-party advertising SDKs, and no analytics beyond the crash reporting and usage events described above.

10. Service Providers

Running this platform means data passes through a small number of other companies. Naming them is more useful than a general assurance, so here they are and what each one sees:

  • Cloudflare hosts this website, serves its data files, and runs the two services behind the on-site assistant and the developer API. Like any web host it processes the requests needed to serve a page.
  • Groq runs the language model that writes the assistant’s short explanatory answers, on this site and in the apps. Every question is answered first from our own published research; only a question that asks for an explanation is also sent to Groq, together with the published readings it concerns, so that it can write a short answer on top. On this site, up to a few of your previous turns in that conversation go with it. Nothing identifying you is sent, and the conversation is held in your own browser session or in the app on your device, not in a database of ours.
  • Google provides several separate things: the YouTube API described in Sections 1 and 2, Firebase for the mobile apps’ notifications, crash reporting and usage events described in Section 8, the Firebase database this website reads live prices from, and the web fonts this site loads.
  • No advertising or tracking companies. This site runs no analytics script, no advertising network, no tag manager and no session-recording tool. That is not an oversight; there is deliberately nothing of the kind in the page.
