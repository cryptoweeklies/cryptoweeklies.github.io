What the badge shows

Risk Level is the 1 to 10 band of the asset's Gravity Risk score, which measures how far the price has stretched above its lifetime Time-Weighted Average Price (TWAP). How Gravity Risk is built. The badge reads the same figures as the report pages and the JSON API.
