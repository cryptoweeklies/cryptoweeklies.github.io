Methodology & Description

The Peak/Valley Barometer evaluates time-series risk across cryptocurrency assets using two distinct mathematical models, allowing users to toggle between leading structural indicators and lagging statistical confirmations.

  • Leading Engine (Structural Extension): Built on absolute structural vectors (Drawdown from ATH and 200-day Simple Moving Average Variance). This engine acts as a leading indicator, highly effective at predicting blow-off euphoric tops and immediate capitulation plunges (e.g., June 2022).
  • Lagging Engine (Statistical Exhaustion): Built strictly on historical quantitative metrics (Expanding Alpha, Beta, Sharpe Ratio, Diminishing Volatility, and baseline Drawdown). This engine maps directly to traditional financial risk calculations, making it highly effective at confirming multi-month apathy bottoms (e.g., Nov/Dec 2022).

By adaptively scaling the data over rolling volatility regimes, the index perfectly accounts for the asset class maturing, ensuring smaller recent drawdowns trigger the same capitulation boundaries as historical crashes.
