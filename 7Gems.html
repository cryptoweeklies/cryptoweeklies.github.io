Mathematical Engine & Methodology

1. Cycle-Adjusted Gravity Index (G) Motivation: Measure market structure strictly against the current liquidity cycle, filtering out historical 'Dino Coin' noise.
Logic: An asset's current deviation from its Cycle TWAP (since Jan 1, 2023) is measured purely against its own historical absolute maximum and minimum stretch in this cycle. 50 is equilibrium. This actively penalizes assets from prior cycles that have failed to generate new momentum, naturally excluding them from the Breakout zones.
G = [ (Δcurrent - Δmin) / (Δmax - Δmin) ] × 100
2. Composite Risk Score (Y-Axis) Motivation: Pure gravity is noisy. A micro-cap resting at a Gravity of 80 is fundamentally far riskier than Bitcoin resting at a Gravity of 80.
Logic: We scale true systemic risk by dividing Raw Gravity by a Logarithmic Dominance Multiplier. Bitcoin maintains a baseline of 1.0x, while micro-caps receive a heavy division penalty (bounded at 0.15). To prevent dead "Dino Coins" from appearing low-risk simply because their gravity is 0, we enforce a Base Risk Floor of 10.
Risk = max(G, 10) ÷ Dominance
3. Bubble Size (Impact Score) Motivation: Visually identify which assets are genuinely moving the market right now.
Logic: The physical size of the bubble is completely decoupled from the Risk calculations. It simply multiplies current Logarithmic Volume, Absolute 90D Momentum, and the Gravity Weight to highlight active liquidity flow, letting market data dictate the size natively.
Size = log10(Vol30D) × √(|Ret90D| + 1) × Gweight
Relative Matrix Assignment (Cross-Sectional Quadrants)
Logic: Quadrants are strictly Relative. An asset's zone is determined by comparing its Momentum and Risk against the Median of all 50 assets. This eliminates "false signals" when the entire macro market is bleeding.
Golden Breakout: Momentum > Median & Risk < Median. Historically cheap against its peers but currently absorbing capital fast. A new markup phase.
Euphoria (Red): Momentum > Median & Risk > Median. Leading the market, but structurally overextended and highly susceptible to mean-reversion.
Deep Accumulation: Momentum < Median & Risk < Median. Cheap but bleeding. The "boring" zone representing deep consolidation and bottoms.
Value Trap (Purple): Momentum < Median & Risk > Median. Dead momentum, yet mathematically hovering in a high-risk premium tier. Stagnation.
