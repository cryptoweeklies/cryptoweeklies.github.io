How this page is built

Every automated refresh since 2024 committed the regenerated report pages to this site's repository, so version history doubles as a daily archive. This page replays that archive: for each day it reopens the BTC DCA regression chart and the Peak/Valley Risk Oscillator exactly as they were published, and reads the day's closing price, risk band, market zone and the regression's own fair-value estimate off the archived chart itself. The DCA model is the one worth archiving: its regression is refit as new data arrives, so what it asserted on a given day cannot be reconstructed from today's page — unlike the TWAP baseline, which is deterministic and needs no archive. Readings are never recomputed from today's data, so later refits cannot rewrite what was said at the time.
