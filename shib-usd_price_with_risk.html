Shiba Inu 200-week moving average: where's the cycle bottom?

Description:

This plot shows the SHIB-USD price (in USD) on a logarithmic scale, with colors representing normalized risk levels.

The risk is calculated as the distance between the closing price and the 20-week Simple Moving Average (SMA), normalized by dividing by the maximum distance observed. Negative distances represent lower risk, and positive distances represent higher risk.
