📊 Mathematical Methodology: Diminishing Volatility Regression

This chart visualizes the mathematical trajectory of COTI using a 2nd-degree polynomial regression fitted to the natural logarithm of its historical price. Because exponential growth naturally decays over time as an asset matures, a 2nd-degree polynomial (a gentle curve) provides a highly accurate model of this "diminishing returns" adoption curve.

Dynamic Standard Deviations (The Volatility Funnel)

As market capitalization grows from millions to billions (and trillions), it requires exponentially more capital to move the price. Therefore, drawing standard deviation bands with a static, fixed variance over a 15-year period is statistically flawed. This model utilizes a Diminishing Volatility Multiplier, mathematically forcing the standard deviation bands to compress and funnel inward from inception to 2030, mirroring the historical maturation of the asset class.

Understanding the Bands

The thick White line represents the Fair Value Baseline. The outer bands represent Dynamic Standard Deviations from this mean:

  • Upper Bands (+1 to +3 Std Dev): Zones of increasing euphoria. Hitting the red +3 line historically correlates with cycle "blow-off" tops.
  • Radioactive Green (-1 Std Dev): Undervaluation zone. Often acts as support during mid-cycle corrections.
  • Dark Green (-2 Std Dev): Deep Value zone. Historically acts as the ultimate floor during standard bear markets.
  • Radiant Blue (-3 Std Dev): Maximum Capitulation. A statistical black swan. Entering this zone represents generational accumulation.
