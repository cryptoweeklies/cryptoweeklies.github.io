How the calculation works

CAGR = (end price / start price) raised to (365.2425 / elapsed calendar days), minus 1. Multiply by 100 for a percentage. Dates and prices come from the observed Price Trend in the Bitcoin chart. Only observed prices are used; forecast lines are excluded. A trailing window uses the last available reading on or before its anniversary, within seven days. A start-year window uses the first reading on or after January 1, within seven days. Start-year windows require at least 30 elapsed days. Windows missing more than 10% of daily observations are omitted.

The current start-year row may cover less than one year; it is annualized, not a full-year return. CAGR smooths the path between two endpoints and hides drawdowns and volatility. It excludes fees, taxes, inflation and trading costs. It is sensitive to the chosen dates.
