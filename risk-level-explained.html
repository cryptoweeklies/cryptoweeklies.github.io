What does Risk Level 1 to 10 mean?

Risk Level is the Gravity Risk score shown in ten bands. Level 1 is the coolest tenth of the scale and Level 10 the hottest.

How is Risk Level calculated?

Gravity Risk runs from 0 to 100, and the chart colours each reading by the tenth of the scale it falls in: roughly 0 to 10 is Level 1 and 90 to 100 is Level 10. The edges sit just above each multiple of ten, at 10.01, 20.02 and so on, so a score of exactly 10 is still Level 1.

Levels are a finer cut than the score's four bands, which change at 25, 50 and 75, and three levels cross a band edge: Level 3 (20.02 to 30.03) spans 25, Level 8 (70.07 to 80.08) spans 75, and Level 5 (40.04 to 50.05) reaches a hair past 50. The stock charts cut each level on the multiple of ten itself, so there only Level 3 and Level 8 cross one.

How do you read a Risk Level?

Exactly as you read Gravity Risk, in coarser steps. The colours run from teal at Level 1 to red at Level 10. A level compares an asset with its own history: Level 7 on two coins means each is equally stretched against its own lifetime average, not that they are equally risky.

What can a Risk Level not tell you?

Everything Gravity Risk cannot. A high level can persist through a long rally, and the crypto and stock Gravity models use different baselines, so a coin's level and a stock's level are not directly comparable.
