🧠 The Intuition & Action Pills

The engine changes only one thing: how much of the next paycheck gets deployed at the current price regime.

Reserve Skipped or reduced buys stay as cash. Crash weeks can then deploy more than one paycheck without pretending new money appeared.
Actual Buy The buy is capped by available cash plus the new paycheck. A 2.5x signal only executes in full when the reserve can fund it.

📐 The S.O.T.A Math Engine

The explicit mathematical formulas driving Approach A.

1. Expanding Lifetime Cost Basis (TWAPt)
TWAPt = 1 / t × ∑i=1t [ Pricei ]
Establishes the true, unmanipulated lifetime anchor of the digital asset.
2. Bounded 2-Year Volatility Squeeze (Zt)
Zt = ( Devt − μ104 ) / σ104
Why 104 Weeks? Because 104 weeks equals exactly 2 years in weekly periodicity. Crypto undergoes asymptotic volatility compression. If we compare 2026 prices against 2017 extremes, modern charts look permanently safe. Squeezing deviations through a rolling 104-week standard deviation ignores ancient history and correctly flags Levels 1-10 Risk relative to the current macro cycle.
3. Stateful Reality Clamping Function (Approach A)
ActualBuyt = min( Paycheck × Mult(Zt), Poolt−1 + Paycheck )
Example: With a $50 weekly paycheck and $100 reserve, a 2.5x crash signal wants $125. Available cash is $150, so the full $125 buy can execute.
