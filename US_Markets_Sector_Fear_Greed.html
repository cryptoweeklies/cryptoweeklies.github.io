How it is computed

A 0–100 mood gauge for the U.S. Stock Market sector: breadth across its stocks, and the trend, volatility and drawdown of S&P 500 SPDR ETF. The six parts above are weighted 24/18/18/18/12/10, summed and clipped to 0–100. Under 20 is Extreme Fear, 20–40 Fear, 40–60 Neutral, 60–80 Greed and 80 or more Extreme Greed.

It is the sector's own index, not CNN's or the crypto Fear & Greed index.
