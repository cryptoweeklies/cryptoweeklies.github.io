Support

CryptoWeeklies — website, iOS and Android apps

CryptoWeeklies is an educational market-analytics dashboard: risk indicators, mathematical models, comparison tools and weekly structural readings for crypto and stock universes. This page is the support contact point for the website and the mobile apps.

Contact

For questions, bug reports, feature requests or data queries, email us. We read everything and aim to reply within a few business days.

cryptoweeklies@gmail.com

Reporting a problem with the app

The fastest way to get a fix is to include:

  • Your device model and OS version (e.g. iPhone 15, iOS 26).
  • The screen you were on and what you tapped last.
  • A screenshot, if the problem is visual.
  • The date and rough time — our data refreshes on a schedule, and knowing when helps us match the feed you were looking at.

Common questions

  • How often does the data update? The feeds refresh daily; the weekly readings are published on the site each week.
  • Is this financial advice? No. Everything in the app and on the site is educational, quantitative research — not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
  • Do I need an account? No. There are no accounts, sign-ins or subscriptions.
  • A number in the app disagrees with the website. The app and site read the same feed, but they may have fetched it at different times. Pull to refresh in the app; if it still disagrees, email us the two screenshots.
