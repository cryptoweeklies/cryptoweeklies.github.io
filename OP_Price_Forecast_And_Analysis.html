SARIMAX Forecasting Methodology

This analysis provides a projection of OP's valuation against the US Dollar (USD) using a Seasonal ARIMA model combined with a Box-Cox transformation to normalize historical volatility.

  • Base Case: The mathematical mean trajectory projected by the algorithm.
  • Bull Case: The upper bound of the confidence interval, representing an optimistic outperformance.
  • Poly Channel (±2σ): A quadratic log-price regression envelope spanning history and the full 23-week forecast horizon. Only the two-standard-deviation channel is shown.
