Crypto Risk Analysis Terminal

Analyzing Volatility, Alpha, Beta, Sortino & Drawdown

Widget 1 Analysis (The Quadrants):
  • 💎 Gem Zone (Top-Left): The holy grail. High historical returns with low volatility.
  • 🎢 Volatile Growth (Top-Right): High returns, but you endured wild swings to get them.
  • ⚓ Stable/Boring (Bottom-Left): Low risk, but low returns. Capital preservation zone.
  • 💀 Danger Zone (Bottom-Right): The worst of both worlds. High risk and poor returns.
Widget 2 Analysis (Portfolio DNA):
This chart visualizes the aggregate strength of your final, cap-weighted "Gem Hunter" portfolio.
How to read: The shape represents the weighted average score (0.0-1.0) of all coins in your portfolio for each metric.
A large, outward-pushing shape means the portfolio as a whole has strong metrics (e.g., High Alpha, Low Max Drawdown, High Sortino).
Widget 3 Analysis (Individual Ranking):
A simplified 0-1 risk score for each individual asset, combining all 5 metrics equally.
Score ~0.0: Safest historical profile.
Score ~1.0: Riskiest historical profile.
Widget 4 Analysis (Final Allocation):
The top 10 most efficient assets (filtered for junk), weighted heavily by their Market Cap size (Square Root) and Safety Score.
This results in a portfolio that holds high-quality assets but allocates significantly more capital to the larger, more established ones among them.
