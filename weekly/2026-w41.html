How to read this

A week's figure is the last reading the site published inside that ISO week, Monday to Sunday, with that reading's own date; a crypto reading is usually dated Sunday and a stock reading Friday. A reading filed under a week it is not dated in is left out, and so is any asset whose data is paused. Week-over-week counts are made on the assets with a reading inside both weeks. The crypto TWAP is lifetime; the stock and ETF TWAP starts in January 2020, or on the first trading day of one listed later. Gravity Risk bands are 25, 50 and 75, read from the score as printed.
