How to read this

Each row counts weeks, and neighbouring weeks share most of their 26-week window, so a band the model read for twenty weeks in a row is closer to one episode than to twenty separate tests. A week counts toward a later change only if a reading was published within seven days of the 13- or 26-week date. The bands are the ones each week's page showed at the time, recovered from the site's own published history, and each price is the price on that same page. Bands with fewer than 4 complete windows are counted but not summarised.
