SARIMAX Forecasting Methodology

The forecast engine resamples daily prices into weekly structure, stabilizes variance through Box-Cox transformation, fits a SARIMAX trend model, then converts the forecast and confidence intervals back into price space. The log-scale y-axis uses a fixed clean tick ladder so the chart reads consistently across low-priced and high-priced equities.

  • Bear 99.7% / Bear 95% / Bear 68%: downside scenario bands.
  • Base: the model's central forecast path.
  • Bull 68% / Bull 95% / Bull 99.7%: upside scenario bands.

The 68% band is the primary operating range; the 95% and 99.7% bands are wider stress envelopes for unusually strong regime outcomes.
