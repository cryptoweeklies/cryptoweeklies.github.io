Capital Rotation Methodology

Capital in cryptocurrency generally flows in a cycle: Fiat → Bitcoin → Large Caps → Mid/Small Caps. This dashboard mathematically detects where capital is positioned by tracking the 90-Day Returns of the Top 50 Altcoins against Bitcoin. If 75% or more of Altcoins outperform Bitcoin over a 90-day window, the market is in "Altcoin Season" (Risk-On). If the index drops below 25%, the market has fled to the safety of "Bitcoin Season" (Risk-Off).
