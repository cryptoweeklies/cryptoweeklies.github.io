SARIMAX Forecasting Methodology

This analysis provides a forecast of LTC prices using a Seasonal ARIMA model with Box-Cox transformation.

  • Base Case: The average forecasted trajectory.
  • Bull Case: The upper bound of the confidence interval, representing an optimistic scenario.

The confidence levels used are 68%, 95%, and 99.7%. This acts as a mathematical cone illustrating the probability that the actual price will fall within the Bear and Bull bounds over the next 23 weeks.
