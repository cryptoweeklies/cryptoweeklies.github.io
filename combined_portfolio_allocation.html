S.O.T.A Mathematical Engine & Methodology

1. The Efficient Frontier (Scatter Matrix)Motivation: Visually plot raw risk against historical reward to find mispriced assets.
Logic: The X-Axis represents Annualized Volatility (Risk). The Y-Axis represents Annualized Returns. Assets that push high and to the left (Gem Zone) are generating massive returns with minimal mathematical volatility.
2. Sortino Ratio (Z-Axis / Scatter Size)Motivation: Standard Sharpe ratios punish assets for upside pumping. We only care about downside risk.
Logic: Physical bubble size is driven by a bounded Sortino Ratio. Explicitly measures excess return divided by downside deviation.
3. Dynamic Robo-Advisor (Portfolio Weights)Motivation: Translate raw metrics into customizable allocation strategy.
Logic: Sizing adapts dynamically via the real-time Risk Slider.
